About the college

Learning with purpose

Munali Hills College of Biotechnology empowers students through quality education and practical experience in Lusaka, Zambia.

Our mission

To innovate, preserve, and disseminate knowledge while promoting a culture of broad inquiry throughout and beyond the MHC community.

Our vision

To be a widely recognised innovative college with global impact.

Our values

We provide accessible education while maintaining high standards of academic and professional excellence.

ExcellenceIntegrityRelevanceInnovationInclusivity

Accreditation & recognition

Munali Hills College of Biotechnology is accredited by the Higher Education Board and TEVETA (Technical Education, Vocational and Entrepreneurship Training Authority). Diploma and short-course programmes are eligible for CDF (Constituency Development Fund) sponsorship.

Admissions

Your next step starts here

Prepare your academic documents and identification, then complete the online application. The admissions team will review your submission and contact you about the next steps.

Apply online

  1. Choose a programme

    Review the available study pathways and select your programme of interest.

  2. Complete the application

    Provide your details and upload the requested supporting documents.

  3. Pay the application fee and wait for review

    You will receive an email confirmation. Admissions staff will review your application and contact you with the decision.

  4. Register after approval

    Complete both physical and online registration after your application is approved.

Before you apply

  • Choose your programme of interest.
  • Prepare your academic documents and identification.
  • Complete the online application and submit the requested documents.
  • Pay the application fee and check your email for confirmation.
