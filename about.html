Accreditation & Recognition

Munali Hills College of Biotechnology is HIGHER EDUCATION BOARD and TEVETA (Technical Education, Vocational and Entrepreneurship Training Authority) accredited, ensuring our programs meet national standards for technical education. All our diploma and short course programs are recognized and eligible for CDF (Constituency Development Fund) Sponsorship, making quality education accessible and affordable for all deserving students.
